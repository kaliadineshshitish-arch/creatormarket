# CreatorMarket

One place for creators: **buy ready-made digital products with instant download**, or **make your own with free AI tools**.

**Live site:** https://creatormarket-nu.vercel.app

## What it does

- **Digital store** — templates, AI prompt packs and eBooks with instant download after payment.
- **Free AI tools** — [AI prompt generator](https://creatormarket-nu.vercel.app/ai-prompt-generator),
  YouTube video analyzer, and a social content studio.
- **Payments** — UPI for India, PayPal for international buyers. No card details are ever stored.
- **Creator dashboard** — list a product, upload a photo and the file, track sales.

## Stack

- Static HTML/CSS/JS front end on **Vercel**
- **Supabase** for auth, Postgres (row-level security) and storage
- **Supabase Edge Functions** for order creation, payment verification and AI calls
- **Sarvam AI** for prompt and content generation
