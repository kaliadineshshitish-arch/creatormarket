Why most resumes get rejected before a human reads them

Because a machine reads them first, and it is looking for keywords.

Most Indian companies above a certain size run applications through an applicant tracking system. It does not judge whether you are good. It checks whether your resume contains the words in the job description. If the posting says "Google Analytics" and your resume says "analytics tools", it scores you low and a human never sees the file.

That is the whole problem this tool solves. You paste the job description, and the builder writes a resume that uses the same language the posting uses — and then tells you which keywords you are still missing, honestly.

How it works

  • 1. Fill the form once. Name, target role, education, work experience, skills. Plain language is fine — you do not need to write it well.
  • 2. Paste the job description. This is optional but it is the part that makes the ATS score real. Without it you get a generic resume and a capped score.
  • 3. Pick a language. English for corporate roles, Hinglish for startups and sales, Hindi for government and local roles.
  • 4. Get the resume plus a score. One page, ATS-friendly, no tables or columns. Along with it: matched keywords, missing keywords and the top three fixes.
  • 5. Download. Print / Save PDF, one page, no watermark, no site branding on the resume itself.

What makes this one different

  • It will not invent experience. If you leave something out, you get a visible [ADD: ...] placeholder instead of a fabricated degree or company. That matters, because a lie on a resume is a fireable offence later.
  • It scores honestly. A resume with no job description supplied cannot score above 70. The number means something because it is allowed to be low.
  • Hinglish is a real option. Not a translated English resume. Job titles stay in English, everything else reads the way an Indian professional actually writes.
  • It is genuinely free. No signup, no card, no trial that expires. 10 generations a month across all the tools.

Questions

Is the AI resume builder really free?

Yes. It is free, with no signup and no payment. You get 10 AI generations a month across all CreatorMarket tools, which is enough for a handful of resumes and rewrites.

Does it really check ATS?

It scores your resume against the job description you paste in, lists the keywords that match, and lists the ones you are missing. That is the same signal an applicant tracking system uses when it filters resumes. If you do not paste a job description, it will not score above 70, because there is nothing to match against.

Can I write my resume in Hindi or Hinglish?

Yes. There is a language picker with English, Hinglish and Hindi. Hinglish keeps job titles and tool names in English and writes everything else the way Indian professionals actually write, which reads better than a literal translation.

Will it invent experience I do not have?

No. If you leave out your education, company names or numbers, it inserts a clear [ADD: ...] placeholder instead of guessing. You have to fill those in yourself, which is also the honest thing to do.

Can I download the resume as a PDF?

Yes. Use the Print / Save PDF button, then choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. It prints on one page with no site branding on it.

Is my data stored?

The details you type are sent to the AI to generate the resume and are not saved on our servers. The free-usage counter is stored in your browser, not tied to your identity.
