Privacy Policy

How CreatorMarket collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated: 23 October 2026.

✓ Your data, explained✓ No data selling✓ Cookies & analytics

1. Who we are

CreatorMarket (https://creatormarket-nu.vercel.app) is an online marketplace for digital products.

This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. Last updated: 23 October 2026.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see your password in plain text).
  • Listing information: product titles, descriptions, prices and links you add when selling.
  • Order information: order ID, product, amount and payment status. We do not receive your card, UPI or netbanking details.
  • Reviews: the rating and text you submit.
  • Usage data: basic analytics about how visitors use the site.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process orders and show you your purchases.
  • To operate the marketplace (listings, reviews, seller stats).
  • To improve the site and its tools.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use basic cookies/local storage to keep you logged in and to remember preferences. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are popular). Google Analytics may set its own cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Cashfree — payment processing (UPI and cards).
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics.
  • AI providers — to power the creator tools you choose to use.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above as needed to run the site, or where required by law.

7. Data retention

We keep your account information while your account is active, and for as long as needed to meet legal and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

8. Your rights

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data.
  • Contact us at creatormarket.support@gmail.com for any of the above.

9. Children

CreatorMarket is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

11. Contact

Privacy questions? Email creatormarket.support@gmail.com.
