What makes an image prompt work

Vague prompts give vague images.

A strong image prompt names the subject, the style, the lighting, the lens or angle, the mood, and the aspect ratio. Leave any of those out and the model guesses — usually badly.

A word on taste

Be specific about what you want — and respectful about what you depict.

Religious, cultural and real-person imagery deserves care. Describe the scene respectfully and avoid anything misleading or defamatory.
