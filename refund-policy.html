Refund & Cancellation Policy

When and how refunds work on CreatorMarket. Last updated: 23 October 2026.

✓ Digital products✓ Clear rules✓ 7-day request window

1. Digital products

All products on CreatorMarket are digital products (files, templates, prompt packs and similar). Because they are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, they are generally non-refundable once delivered.

2. When a refund IS available

  • Not delivered: you paid but never received the product or download link.
  • Defective file: the file is corrupt or does not open, and the seller cannot fix it.
  • Duplicate or wrong charge: you were charged twice, or charged for the wrong item.
  • Unauthorised payment: a payment you did not make.
  • Significantly not as described: the product is materially different from its description.

3. When a refund is NOT available

  • Change of mind after the product has been delivered/downloaded.
  • The product works as described and matches its listing.
  • You no longer need the product.

4. How to request a refund

  • Email creatormarket.support@gmail.com within 7 days of purchase.
  • Include your order ID, the product name, and a short reason (with a screenshot if helpful).
  • We will review the request and reply, usually within 3–5 working days.

5. Cancellations

Because digital products are delivered instantly, orders cannot normally be cancelled after payment. If a product has not yet been delivered, contact us and we will do our best to help.

6. Refund processing time

Approved refunds are sent back to your original payment method. Banks and payment providers usually take 5–7 working days to credit the amount.

7. Contact

For any refund or cancellation question, email creatormarket.support@gmail.com.
