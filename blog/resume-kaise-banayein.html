Resume Kaise Banayein: ATS Guide for Indian Job Seekers

Aap 50 applications bhejte ho aur ek bhi reply nahi aata. Reason yeh nahi hai ki aap capable nahi ho. Reason yeh hai ki aapka resume ek machine tak pahunchta hai, aur woh machine usse reject kar deti hai — insaan ne dekha bhi nahi.

ATS kya hai, aur woh kya dekhta hai

ATS matlab Applicant Tracking System. Badi companies — Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Amazon, Flipkart — sab ise use karti hain. Jab aap unki careers site par resume upload karte ho, woh file ek software me chali jati hai jo usse padhta hai aur job description se match karta hai.

Woh software sirf do cheezein dekhta hai: keywords aur structure. Aapka college, aapki mehnat, aapki personality — kuch nahi. Agar job description me "Google Analytics" likha hai aur aapke resume me "analytics tools" likha hai, to score kam aayega.

Yeh unfair lagta hai, aur hai bhi. Par isse ladne ka tarika yeh nahi ki aap zyada applications bhejo. Tarika yeh hai ki aap us machine ki bhasha me likho.

Woh 5 galtiyan jo sab karte hain

1. Ek hi resume har jagah bhejna

Yeh sabse badi galti hai. Har job description alag keywords maangti hai. Ek resume jo 20 jagah bheja gaya, woh 20 jagah average lagega. Ek resume jo us ek job ke liye likha gaya, woh select hoga.

Fix: har application ke liye 10 minute do. Job description padho, usme se 8-10 keywords nikalo, aur apne resume me wahi shabd use karo — sirf tab jab woh sach ho.

2. Table aur column me likhna

Kai log resume ko sundar dikhane ke liye do column me banate hain. ATS usse galat padhta hai — text mix ho jata hai, aur aapka experience bekar nikalta hai.

Fix: ek column. Simple. Sirf headings, bullets aur plain text.

3. Photo, age, marital status, father's name

Yeh Indian resume ki purani aadat hai. Private companies me iska nuksan hai — kai ATS photo ko text samajh ke confuse ho jate hain, aur kai companies bias se bachne ke liye aise resume skip kar deti hain.

Fix: naam, phone, email, city, LinkedIn. Bas itna. Photo sirf sarkari form me.

4. Kaam likhna, result nahi

"Handled social media" — yeh kaam hai. "Grew Instagram from 400 to 12,000 in 8 months" — yeh result hai. Recruiter result padhta hai.

Fix: har bullet me ek number daalo. Kitna, kitne me, kitno ke saath.

5. PDF ke bajaye Word file bhejna

Word file ka layout har computer par badal jata hai. Aapne jo sundar banaya tha, woh recruiter ke laptop par toota hua dikhta hai.

Fix: hamesha PDF. Naam me apna naam rakho — Dinesh-Kalia-Resume.pdf, resume-final-final.pdf nahi.

Step by step: resume kaise banayein

Step 1: Job description se keywords nikalo

Job posting copy karo. Usme se woh shabd highlight karo jo skills ya tools hain — Excel, Tally, SEO, Python, customer service, Hindi, English. 8-12 keywords milenge.

Step 2: Apna dhancha banao

Order yeh rakho: naam aur contact, summary (2 line), skills (ek line), experience (naya pehle), education. Bas.

Step 3: Summary me apna sabse bada result likho

Do line. Pehli line — aap kaun ho aur kitne saal. Doosri line — aapka sabse bada result, number ke saath.

Sales professional with 3 years in FMCG. Grew territory revenue from ₹18L to ₹31L in 14 months across 40+ retail accounts.

Step 4: Har bullet me number daalo

Kam se kam 3 bullet per job. Har bullet me ek number — kitna, kitne me, kitno ke saath. Agar number yaad nahi, andaaza lagao aur likho "approximately". Jhooth mat likho.

Step 5: Skills me wahi shabd use karo jo JD me hain

Job description me "MS Excel" likha hai to "Excel" mat likho — "MS Excel" likho. ATS exact match dhundhta hai.

Step 6: PDF me export karo, ek page, saaf

Font 10-11, margin theek, koi design nahi. Ek page me sab aa jana chahiye.

Step 7: Score check karo, phir bhejo

Bhejne se pehle dekho ki aapka resume job description se kitna match karta hai. Agar 3-4 keywords missing hain aur woh sach me aapke paas hain, to add karo. Agar nahi hain, to us job ke liye aap galat candidate ho — aur usme koi sharam nahi.

Hinglish me resume — kab chalta hai

Hinglish ka matlab yeh nahi ki aap tooti-phooti English likho. Matlab yeh hai: job titles, tools aur company names English me, aur baaki baat us tarah likhi jaye jaise Indian professionals actually likhte hain.

Yeh startups, sales, marketing aur local businesses me chalta hai. MNC, IT services aur banking me English hi bhejo. Sarkari jobs me Hindi ya English — jo form maange.

Kya ATS score 100 hona chahiye

Nahi. 100 ka matlab hai aapne har keyword daal diya — jisme kuch relevant nahi the. 70-85 achha score hai. Uske baad insaan padhta hai, aur wahan aapke numbers bolte hain.

Aur yaad rakho: ATS aapko reject nahi karta. ATS aapko rank karta hai. Top 20 me aana hai. Uske baad sab barabar hai.

Questions

Resume kitne page ka hona chahiye?

Ek page. Fresher ho ya 10 saal ka experience — India me ek page hi padha jata hai. Do page tabhi theek hain jab aapke paas 8+ saal ka experience ho aur usme alag-alag companies ho.

Kya photo lagani chahiye?

Private companies me nahi. ATS software photo padh nahi sakta, aur kai companies bias se bachne ke liye photo wale resume reject kar deti hain. Sirf sarkari jobs me photo maangi jati hai — wahan form ke saath.

Hinglish me resume bana sakte hain?

Startups, sales aur local businesses me chalta hai — jahan English achhi nahi maangi jati. MNC, IT aur banking me English hi bhejo. Hindi medium wale sarkari jobs me Hindi me bana sakte hain.

Experience nahi hai to kya likhein?

Projects, college ka kaam, freelance, internship, aur volunteer kaam. Ek line bhi likhna behtar hai 'Kuch nahi' se. Par jhooth mat likho — background check me pakda jata hai.

Salary expectation likhni chahiye?

Nahi. Pehle interview me discuss karo. Resume me number likhne se ya to aap kam maang rahe ho ya zyada — dono me nuksan hai.

ATS score 100 kab hota hai?

Jab aapki resume ka har important keyword job description se match kare. Par 100 ka matlab job pakka nahi — uske baad insaan padhta hai. 70-85 achha score hai.
