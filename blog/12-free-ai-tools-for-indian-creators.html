Kaunse teen se shuru karo

Agar aapko pata nahi kahan se shuru karein, to yeh teen sabse zyada kaam aate hain:

1. AI Resume Builder. Agar aap job dhundh rahe ho, yeh sabse pehla tool hai. Ek form, ek job description, aur aapko pata chal jata hai ki aapka resume kahan kamzor hai — guess karne ki zaroorat nahi.

2. Business Name Generator. Naya business ya side project shuru kar rahe ho? 10 naam, 30 second me. Kam se kam ek to pasand aayega.

3. Brand Deal Rate Calculator. Agar koi brand aapko message karta hai aur aapko nahi pata kitna maangein — yeh tool pehle kholo, phir reply karo.

Yeh tools kya nahi karte

Honest rehna zaroori hai.

Yeh aapke liye kaam nahi karte. Resume builder aapki experience khud nahi banata — jo aapne diya, usse likhta hai. Jahan kuch missing hai, wahan [ADD: ...] likh deta hai, jhooth nahi bolta.

Yeh rank guarantee nahi karte. YouTube title generator keyword-first title deta hai, par video rank karega ya nahi — woh content aur competition par hai.

Yeh data save nahi karte. Resume builder jo aap type karte ho, woh save nahi hota. Tool use karo, tab band karo, sab gaya.
