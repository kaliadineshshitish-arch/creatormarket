FAQ

How do payments work?

Two ways. UPI (default — no gateway needed): pay straight to the owner's UPI ID by QR or UPI app; the order is then marked “awaiting confirmation”. Cashfree (when enabled): the order is created on a secure Supabase Edge Function and verified server-side before it is marked paid. Secret keys never touch the browser.

Do you store my card details?

No. Card, UPI and netbanking details are entered on Cashfree's PCI-compliant checkout. CreatorMarket only ever sees an order ID and payment status.

Is login real?

Yes. Accounts use Supabase Auth with email and password. Your password is never stored in this page.

Can CreatorMarket publish to Instagram, Facebook or TikTok?

The workspace generates platform-specific content. Direct publishing needs each platform's official API/OAuth setup; CreatorMarket will not fake a connection or ask for your social passwords.

How many free AI generations do I get?

10 free AI generations per calendar month in the Prompt Creator. The browser counter resets monthly; it is a convenience limit, not a security boundary.
